Privacy and transparency

TAI2ME runs the questionnaire in your browser. It has no sign-in, database, analytics, ads, forms that submit, or third-party scripts. Your answers are kept only in this page's temporary memory, not saved between visits.

“Connect to your AI” means you deliberately copy the visible learning prompt, open your preferred AI service, and paste it there. This is not an account/API connection. That provider's own privacy policy then applies. Never include passwords, private documents, bank details, or personal identifiers in AI prompts.

The installed app may cache its public program files for offline use. Its public HTML, CSS, and JavaScript can be inspected by anyone; proprietary paid lessons should not be included in the public app.
