Save your AI classroom

Your lessons live in your AI's chat history. TAI2ME cannot see, rename, or find them automatically. You don't need another new chat.

▶ Continue saved classroom

This opens your saved private conversation in your AI account.

  1. In your AI's sidebar, select the existing lesson under Pinned, Recents, or History. Don't choose New chat.
  2. Rename it TAI2ME — My AI Classroom and pin it if your AI supports that.
  3. To make the T2 shortcut reopen the exact chat, open that conversation in a browser and copy the address bar URL (never the Share conversation link).
  4. Return here, paste that private URL, and tap Save classroom shortcut. You only need to do this once per installed TAI2ME app/browser.

Privacy: Do not create or paste a public shared-chat link. We accept only supported private conversation addresses. Your bookmark stays on this device, not on a TAI2ME server.

↗ Open AI to find existing chat

This opens your AI service, not a new TAI2ME class. Select your saved conversation from its history. The direct chat shortcut works only after you save its address below.

A private conversation address is needed for one-tap resume. If your AI doesn't provide one, leave this blank to save its name and follow the history guide instead.

Saved locally in this browser/app only; it won't synchronize across phones or computers. If you install TAI2ME on an iPhone, save the shortcut from the installed app for best results.

Privacy and transparency

TAI2ME runs the questionnaire in your browser. It has no sign-in, database, analytics, ads, forms that submit, or third-party scripts. Your quiz answers remain in temporary page memory and are not saved between visits. If you choose Save classroom shortcut, only your selected AI provider and its direct conversation URL (when supported) are saved in this device's browser storage. You may erase this shortcut at any time.

“Connect to your AI” means you deliberately copy the visible learning prompt, open your preferred AI service, and paste it there. This is not an account/API connection. That provider's own privacy policy then applies. Never include passwords, private documents, bank details, or personal identifiers in AI prompts.

The installed app may cache its public program files for offline use. Its public HTML, CSS, and JavaScript can be inspected by anyone; proprietary paid lessons should not be included in the public app.
